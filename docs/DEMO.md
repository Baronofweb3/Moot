# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

1. **Pitch (20s).** Memecoin launchpads pay the platform. MOOT pays the creator and the trader too, and the rules live in a contract.
2. **Launch (45s).** Open the app, connect a testnet wallet, launch a token. Point out: the creator gets zero tokens, a fixed supply, a $3 launch fee. Add starting liquidity and show the 1-year lock.
3. **Trade (45s).** Buy with a second wallet. Show the quote with the fee breakdown: creator 50%, you 20% (MOOT TIP), treasury 30%.
4. **Earn (30s).** Open the dashboard as the creator and as the trader. Show both balances and withdraw one.
5. **Proof (30s).** Open the Control Room: the solvency check says `reconciles`, and the cumulative fee distribution matches 50/20/30.
6. **Honest close (10s).** Testnet prototype, unaudited. Next: indexer, PNL cards, mainnet hardening.
